Enum EffectType

Namespace
ExecMagica.Engine
Assembly
EngineDocs.dll

Defines what a card effect does.

public enum EffectType

Fields

AddKeyword = 11

Adds a keyword to the selected card. Example: Shield, Provocation, Lifesteal.

BuffAttack = 4

Adds attack to the selected card.

BuffHealth = 5

Adds health to the selected card.

BuffStats = 6

Increases both attack and health of target cards. Uses AttackValue and HealthValue from CardEffect.

DealDamage = 1

Deals damage to the selected target.

DebuffAttack = 7

Reduces attack of the selected card.

Destroy = 9

Destroys the selected card or group of cards.

DrawCards = 3

Draws cards for the effect owner.

GainTemporaryMana = 12

Gives temporary mana for the current turn only. This increases current Mana, but does not increase Manapool. Mana can go above current Manapool, but not above GameRules.MaxManaPool.

GainTemporaryManaNextTurn = 13

Gives temporary mana to the selected hero at the start of their next turn. This does not increase Manapool.

Heal = 2

Heals the selected target.

None = 0

No effect.

Silence = 8

Removes all abilities from the selected card.

Summon = 10

Summons a token card.
