DrawCard(GameState, PlayerState, CardInstance, List<GameEvent>)

Draws the top card into the player's hand, emitting CardDrawn. If the deck is empty the player takes escalating fatigue damage (and may lose); if the hand is full the draw is skipped. Returns the drawn card, or null on fatigue/skip.
