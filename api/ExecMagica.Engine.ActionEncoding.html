Class ActionEncoding

Maps a GameAction to the flat policy index used by the trained network, mirroring ml/train.py:action_index. Slot indices are positions within a zone list, consistent with StateEncoder and SelfPlayDataGenerator.

Flat layout (ActionCount = 227): End=0 | PlayCard(noTarget)=[1..10] | Play→myField=[11..80] | Play→oppField=[81..150] | Play→myHero=[151..160] | Play→oppHero=[161..170] | AttackCard=[171..219] | AttackHero=[220..226]
